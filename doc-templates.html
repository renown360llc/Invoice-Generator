Statement of Work (SOW)
Attached to an MSA for each specific engagement. Defines the candidate, role, location, bill rate, start date, and deliverables for a project.

Used when Renown360 refers a candidate for a referral fee. Documents the candidate, deployment client, bill rate between parties, and non-solicitation terms.
